Shipping and Return Policy

Hariom Garments · Wholesale orders

Shipping arrangements

Provide your delivery PIN code and order quantity for a shipping quote. Confirm freight charges, carrier arrangements and estimated dispatch or delivery timing with the team before ordering.

Reporting order issues

If your shipment is short, damaged or contains the wrong items, contact Hariom Garments promptly with your order reference, package photos and item details. Keep the packaging while the issue is reviewed.

Return arrangements

Check return eligibility, timelines, condition requirements and freight responsibility in writing for your order. Contact the team for return instructions before sending any goods back.
